English Language, as two papers

The whole course is two exams. In each one you read something, then write something. That makes four jobs. Pick one to see what it asks and the tools for it.

English Language

Same thing, different names

The sheets use a lot of labels. Here’s what they mean in plain words.

Paper = component
Same thing. Paper 1 and Component 1 are one exam.
Transactional writing
Writing for real life: an article, a letter, a speech.
Imaginative writing
Writing a story.
Explicit / implicit
Said outright / hinted at. Implicit = reading between the lines.
Objective / subjective
Based on facts / based on opinions.
Logos, ethos, pathos
Facts, experts, feelings.
Story mountain = Freytag’s pyramid
Same diagram, two names.
Denouement = resolution
How the story ends up.
Protagonist
The main character.
Pathetic fallacy
When the weather matches the mood. Nothing to do with “pathetic”.
